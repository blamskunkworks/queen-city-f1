Site information

SECURITY
& PRIVACY.

This page explains how the Queen City F1 website is designed to keep the public experience simple, safe, and privacy-conscious.

01

No visitor accounts

The public website does not require visitors to create an account or sign in.

02

No customer database

The site does not operate contact forms, customer profiles, or a database of visitor-submitted personal information.

03

Read-only race data

Race schedules and championship standings are requested from the public Jolpica F1 data service and displayed read-only.

04

External community links

Links to Instagram, Discord, Signal, and GridRival open their respective services. Their own privacy and security practices apply after you leave this website.

05

Browser protections

The site uses a restrictive content security policy, blocks embedded third-party frames, and prevents external links from controlling the original page.
